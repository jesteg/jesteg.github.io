About

A multidisciplinary academic career connecting mathematical science, medicine, biology, and artificial intelligence.

Jesper Tegnér is Professor and Associate Dean in the Biomedical Sciences Division at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology.

He is affiliated with the Bioscience, Computer Science, Bioengineering, and Statistics programs at KAUST. His research and teaching span bioinformatics, artificial intelligence, computational biology, and technologies for life sciences and medical research.

He holds an MD and a PhD in Experimental and Computational Neuroscience from Karolinska Institutet. His education also encompasses mathematics, theoretical physics, medicine, philosophy, psychology, and postgraduate studies in pure and applied computational mathematics.

Alongside his academic work, he has founded two biotechnology startups and serves as Executive Director at Immune Algorithmics.
